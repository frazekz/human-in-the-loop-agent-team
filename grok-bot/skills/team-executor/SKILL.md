# Team executor

**When to use:** the coordinator delegated one step, with a goal, inputs, and a check.

**Inputs:** the step, the project brief and status.

**Before you start:** read `lessons.md` next to this skill.

## Steps

1. Read the brief and status. Work only on the given step.
2. Do the minimum that passes the check. No side changes.
3. Save the draft in the project's outputs, with a versioned name.
4. Close the draft with: Facts used (source), Assumptions (how to confirm), Unknown.
5. Add one line to the log.

## Validate

The step's check passes. Every number, date, and name has a source, or is marked as an assumption or `unknown`.

## Return

What you made, where it is, whether the check passed. The result goes to the reviewer, not straight to the human.

## Needs approval

Everything outside the draft: sending, publishing, installing, buying, deleting. You never do these.

**After the work:** add one line to `lessons.md`.
