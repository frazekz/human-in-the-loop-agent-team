# Brief — example-bakery-newsletter (fictional)

- **Goal:** a short monthly email newsletter for customers who signed up in the shop.
- **Expected result:** one newsletter draft in markdown, ready to paste into the shop's email tool.
- **Audience:** regular customers of a small neighbourhood bakery.
- **Constraints:** warm, plain tone; under 200 words; one call to action; no discounts promised without the owner's yes.
- **Inputs:** this month's seasonal products, written by the owner (in the decisions file).
- **Done when:** the reviewer returns `clean` and the owner says yes to a test send.
- **Needs a human yes:** sending the newsletter, to anyone.
